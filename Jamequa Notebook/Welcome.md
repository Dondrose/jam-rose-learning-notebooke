## 🧩 Guiding Principles

> [!quote] Operating Rules
> - **Capture, then curate.** Inbox first, structure later.
> - **One dashboard per area.** Always know where to start.
> - **Link, don't file.** Connections beat folders.
> - **Review weekly.** A notebook is only as good as its review loop.
> - **Small and consistent** beats big and sporadic.
